Be Prepared

What to bring & how to show up.

Tryouts can feel intense. Here's exactly what you need so your athlete walks in confident — not scrambling for kneepads in the parking lot.

The checklist.

  • ✓
    Athletic shoes (court only)Clean indoor court shoes — non-marking soles. No outdoor shoes.
  • ✓
    Knee padsRequired. Borrow if you don't have them.
  • ✓
    Water bottleBring more than you think you need. Tryouts are long.
  • ✓
    Hair tied back, comfortable athletic wearSpandex/shorts and a t-shirt. Dress like you're playing, not posing.
  • ✓
    Arrive 30 minutes earlyCheck in, get your number, find your court, breathe.
  • ✓
    A snack & a positive mindsetEat a real meal 2 hours before. Then snack, hydrate, and trust your work.

FAQ

Questions families ask us.

Most-asked tryout and season questions. Don't see yours? Call Patty directly — 816.529.0282.

How does the tryout process work?
Tryouts are skill-based drills run by Club North coaches. Athletes are evaluated on serving, passing, hitting, setting, defense, and game play. Coaches use objective scoring plus team-fit observations. Offers go out within a few hours after tryouts via phone/email.
What does the season look like?
Girls season runs Nov–Jun, boys runs Mar–Jul. Most teams practice twice a week for 2 hours. Tournaments are typically weekends — some local, some travel. Full schedule is shared at your team meeting after tryouts.
How much travel is required?
Travel depends on the team you make. Lower-age and local teams stay in the KC metro. Higher-level travel teams (16U–18U) attend regional and national qualifiers — typically 2–4 travel weekends per season. Family travel is on you, but we help coordinate carpools.
What if my daughter doesn't make her preferred team?
We'll be straight with you about where she landed and why. Every Club North team gets coaching and reps — we don't have "throwaway" teams. Players develop fastest where they're challenged at the right level for them.
Can my child try out for multiple clubs?
Yes — but be aware of HOA Region tryout windows and offer-acceptance timing. If Club North is your first choice, tell us. If we offer and you accept, you're committed for the season. Don't wait on offers from us if you've decided elsewhere.
What if we can't make tryout day?
Email us. We almost always accommodate make-up tryouts for school sports conflicts, family conflicts, or illness. We'd rather see you in a smaller session than miss you entirely.
How are coaches assigned?
Coach assignments are confirmed at the team meeting after offers. We pair coaches based on team age, level, and coaching style. Coach Ib leads our top travel teams; Patty oversees all coaching staff and handles director-level questions.
What's included in the team fee?
Standard club fees cover: practice gym time, coaching, tournament entries (regular season), and team admin. Mizuno team uniform packages are available — exact apparel options are reviewed at team meetings. Travel costs (hotel, food, family transport) are separate.
