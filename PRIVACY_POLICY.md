## The short version
EatOS keeps your information **on your device**. We don't have servers that receive it, we don't sell it, and we don't use it for advertising.

## What EatOS stores on your device
- Your answers from setup: goal, what makes eating hard, foods you like, allergies, diet, budget, and other considerations you choose.
- Meals you accept, save, rate, or hide, so suggestions improve.
- Basic usage counts (for example, how many suggestions you accepted) and error reports if the app crashes.

## What leaves your device
Nothing, automatically. If you tap **Share my usage summary**, your phone's share sheet opens with a text summary of counts and recent error messages. It contains **no allergies, diets, goals, or meal choices**. You choose whether and with whom to share it.

## Health-related information
Allergies, diets, and "medical condition" flags can be sensitive. They stay on your device and are never used for advertising or shared with third parties.

## Deleting your data
Preferences → **Delete all my data** erases everything EatOS stored on your device. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

## Children
EatOS is not directed at children under 13.

## Not medical advice
EatOS suggests general meal ideas. It can't verify restaurant ingredients or kitchen practices and is not a substitute for medical or dietary advice.
